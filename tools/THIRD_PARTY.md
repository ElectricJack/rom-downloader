# Third-party tools

This directory bundles prebuilt third-party command-line tools used by the
ROM post-processing pipelines. They are NOT part of this project and are
distributed under their own respective licenses. All rights belong to their
original authors.

## chdman.exe

- **Project:** MAME (Multiple Arcade Machine Emulator)
- **Source:** https://github.com/mamedev/mame
- **Homepage:** https://www.mamedev.org/
- **License:** BSD-3-Clause (see the MAME project for full terms)
- **Used for:** Converting disc images to/from the compressed CHD format.

## extract-xiso.exe

- **Project:** extract-xiso (maintained by XboxDev)
- **Source:** https://github.com/XboxDev/extract-xiso
- **License:** BSD-2-Clause (see the project for full terms)
- **Used for:** Extracting/repacking Xbox and Xbox 360 ISO images.

If you redistribute this repository, ensure you comply with each tool's
license. To verify integrity or obtain newer versions, download the tools
directly from their official sources above.
